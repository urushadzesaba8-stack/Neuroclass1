## Verification hierarchy
1. Original publication defining the classification or score.
2. Official society guideline / consensus statement, if applicable.
3. Peer-reviewed validation or update paper.
4. Reputable secondary educational source for cross-checking only.

## Rights and attribution
Discovery of a classification name does not grant permission to reproduce a site's text, diagrams, images, or proprietary database. Reconstruct definitions and rules from permitted primary literature, cite sources, and check licenses/terms before importing any material.
